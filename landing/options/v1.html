Quickstart

Your entire onboarding
is this prompt:

Copy this into any AI — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, whatever you use. That's it. That's the whole setup.

Paste into your AI
Read https://api.itslive.dev/llms.txt and deploy a site for me. My email: [your email here]
That's all you paste. The AI reads the spec, generates your site, calls the API, and gives you a live URL. No account. No config. No OAuth.
01
Goes live
instantly.

Deploys in under a second, globally. No build pipelines. No CDN warm-up time. No waiting for DNS propagation. The moment your AI calls the API, the site is live on 200+ edge nodes.

// Median observed deploy latency: 52ms · p95: 340ms
02
No OAuth.
Ever.

AI agents can't click "Authorize App" buttons. So we don't have them. Authentication is a one-time email code — something any agent can handle without human intervention in the loop.

// No API keys to rotate · No sessions to expire · No scopes to misconfigure
